## Connecting the Arducam
Make sure nothing is connected to power when working with electronics. If the Raspberry Pi is on and you modify the electrical connections, it could fry the components. 
{: .warning}

1. Take the Raspberry Pi and Arducam out of their packaging.
2. Take out the heatsinks in the Arducam packaging.
3. Take of the sticky protector on the heatsinks, and attach them to the backside of the Arducam IR Lights on the bronze circles.
    <figure>
        <img src="#" alt="Image of attached heatsinks">
        <figcaption>Heatsinks attached to the Arducam IR light pads.</figcaption>
    </figure>
4. With the Arducam now facing you, pull the black connection point connecting the white ribbon cable in the direction of the paper strip until the black connection point extends slightly.
    <figure class="image-row">
        <div>
            <img src="#" alt="Image of black tab with white ribbon cable">
            <figcaption>Black tab before lifting.</figcaption>
        </div>
        <div>
            <img src="#" alt="Image of black tab lifted">
            <figcaption>Black tab after lifting.</figcaption>
        </div>
    </figure>
5. Take out the white ribbon cable from the connector, and grab the orange ribbon cable in the Arducam packaging. 
6. With the Arducam facing you, insert the rectangular side (wider side) of the orange ribbon cable into the black connection point, so that the gold connectors and the logo are facing towards you. 
    <figure class="image-row">
        <div>
            <img src="#" alt="Arducam and orange ribbon cable in correct orientation">
            <figcaption>Correct orientation of the orange ribbon cable.</figcaption>
        </div>
        <div>
            <img src="#" alt="Orange ribbon cable inserted">
            <figcaption>Cable fully inserted into the connector.</figcaption>
        </div>
    </figure>
7. Close the black connection point and test the mechanical connection with a few light tugs. The orange ribbon cable should not move. 
    <figure>
        <img src="#" alt="Image of closed black connection tab with orange ribbon cable inside">
        <figcaption>Orange ribbon cable correctly installed in the Arducam.</figcaption>
    </figure>
8. Now orient the Raspberry Pi so that it is sitting flat on a surface and the USB ports are facing left. 
9.  Locate the leftmost tan and brown connector (should look like the white and black one on the camera), and lift up the brown connection tab.
    <figure>
        <img src="#" alt="Image of Raspberry Pi facing left with a ribbon cable connector circled">
        <figcaption>Location of ribbon cable connector on Raspberry Pi 5.</figcaption>
    </figure>
10. With the gold contacts facing left, insert the other end of the orange connector into the tan connector until it hits the bottom. It is okay if you can still see the gold connectors on the orange strip. 
    <figure class="image-row">
        <div>
            <img src="#" alt="Image of the orange ribbon cable facing the correct direction">
            <figcaption>Correct orientation of orange ribbon cable in Raspberry Pi.</figcaption>
        </div>
        <div>
            <img src="#" alt="Image of orange ribbon cable correctly installed in the Raspberry Pi">
            <figcaption>Orange ribbon cable fully inserted into Raspberry Pi.</figcaption>
        </div>
    </figure>
11. Push down on the brownish tab to close. Test the mechanical connection again with a few tugs. Once again, the orange paper strip should not move. 
<figure>
    <img src="#" alt="Image of Raspberry Pi and Arducam connected by orange ribbon cable">
    <figcaption>Arducam attached to Raspberry Pi.</figcaption>
</figure>
